# Knowledge retrieval (retrievers, post-retrieval)

* [Answering Complex Open-Domain Questions with Multi-Hop Dense Retrieval](multi-hop-dense-retrieval.md) - Recursive dense retrieval that finds the sequence of passages for multi-hop questions, with the next query formed from the question plus previously retrieved passages, using beam search. (neocortex: adopt · LLM: none)
* [Don't Forget the Base Retriever! A Low-Resource Graph-based Retriever for Multi-hop Question Answering](dont-forget-the-base-retriever.md) - GRiever: an LLM-free, multi-step graph retriever over a passage-triple index plus synonym expansion, running in hundreds of milliseconds. (neocortex: adopt · LLM: indexing)
* [CG-RAG: Research Question Answering by Citation Graph Retrieval-Augmented LLMs](cg-rag.md) - Citation-graph RAG whose LeSeGR retriever combines lexical (sparse) and semantic (dense) signals over query-relevant subgraphs. (neocortex: adapt · LLM: retrieval)
* [G-Retriever: Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Textual Graph Understanding and Question Answering](g-retriever.md) - Retrieves a query-relevant subgraph from a textual graph by solving a prize-collecting Steiner tree problem, then answers with an LLM over the subgraph. (neocortex: adapt · LLM: retrieval)
* [GRAG: Graph Retrieval-Augmented Generation](grag.md) - Retrieves textual subgraphs with a divide-and-conquer ego-graph strategy in linear time, then softly prunes them before LLM generation. (neocortex: adapt · LLM: retrieval)
* [Graph Neural Network Enhanced Retrieval for Question Answering of LLMs](gnn-ret.md) - Builds a passage graph (structure-related or keyword-shared passages) and runs a GNN over retrieval scores; RGNN-Ret extends it across hops. (neocortex: adapt · LLM: retrieval)
* [HippoRAG 2: From RAG to Memory](hipporag-2.md) - Passage-augmented Personalized PageRank over an LLM-built open KG, with an LLM filter on retrieved triples, for non-parametric continual memory. (neocortex: adapt · LLM: both)
* [HybGRAG: Hybrid Retrieval-Augmented Generation on Textual and Relational Knowledge Bases](hybgrag.md) - Combines a bank of text and graph retrievers with a router and a critic that feeds corrective feedback back into retrieval for hybrid questions. (neocortex: adapt · LLM: both)
* [KG2RAG: Knowledge Graph-Guided Retrieval Augmented Generation](kg2rag.md) - Semantic seed chunks are expanded through m-hop neighbourhoods of a KG built over the chunks, then organised into a KG-based context. (neocortex: adapt · LLM: indexing)
* [Knowledge Guided Text Retrieval and Reading for Open Domain Question Answering](kg-guided-passage-graph-retrieval.md) - Open-domain QA that expands keyword-retrieved seed passages by traversing a passage graph whose edges come from a knowledge base or co-occurrence. (neocortex: adapt · LLM: none)
* [LightRAG: Simple and Fast Retrieval-Augmented Generation](lightrag.md) - Graph-structured text index with dual-level (entity-level and relation-level) keyword retrieval over a vector store of entities and relations. (neocortex: adapt · LLM: both)
* [PathRAG: Pruning Graph-based Retrieval Augmented Generation with Relational Paths](pathrag.md) - Retrieves key relational paths between retrieved nodes with flow-based, distance-aware pruning to cut redundancy before prompting. (neocortex: adapt · LLM: both)
* [RAPTOR: Recursive Abstractive Processing for Tree-Organized Retrieval](raptor.md) - Recursively embeds, clusters and summarises chunks into a tree, then retrieves across tree levels at inference. (neocortex: adapt · LLM: indexing)
* [RiTeK: A Dataset for Large Language Models Complex Reasoning over Textual Knowledge Graphs](ritek.md) - A medical textual-KG benchmark with synthesised queries across diverse topological structures, expert-checked. (neocortex: adapt · LLM: none)
* [Advanced RAG Models with Graph Structures: Optimizing Complex Knowledge Reasoning and Text Generation](advanced-rag-gnn.md) - Adds a GNN over graph-structured knowledge to a RAG generator to improve reasoning consistency. (neocortex: watch · LLM: both)
* [Empowering GraphRAG with Knowledge Filtering and Integration](graphrag-fi.md) - GraphRAG-FI filters retrieved graph knowledge in two stages and balances it against the model's own reasoning via logits-based integration. (neocortex: watch · LLM: retrieval)
* [GFM-RAG: Graph Foundation Model for Retrieval Augmented Generation](gfm-rag.md) - A graph neural network trained as a graph foundation model over the knowledge graph, used as a retriever that generalises to unseen datasets without fine-tuning. (neocortex: watch · LLM: retrieval)
* [GraphSearch: An Agentic Deep Searching Workflow for Graph Retrieval-Augmented Generation](graphsearch.md) - Agentic multi-turn GraphRAG with dual-channel retrieval: semantic queries over chunks and relational queries over the graph. (neocortex: watch · LLM: both)
* [HGMEM: Hypergraph-based Working Memory to Improve Multi-step RAG for Long-Context Complex Relational Modeling](hgmem.md) - Working memory as a hypergraph whose hyperedges group related facts and evolve by update, insertion and merge operations during multi-step RAG. (neocortex: watch · LLM: both)
* [KnowGPT: Knowledge Graph based PrompTing for Large Language Models](knowgpt.md) - Extracts the most informative KG knowledge with an RL-based path policy and a bandit over extraction and prompt templates, then turns it into a hard prompt for closed LLMs. (neocortex: watch · LLM: both)
* [Knowledge Graph-Augmented Language Models for Knowledge-Grounded Dialogue Generation](surge.md) - SURGE retrieves a relevant KG subgraph for dialogue and enforces fact consistency with contrastive learning. (neocortex: watch · LLM: none)
* [LightPROF: A Lightweight Reasoning Framework for Large Language Model on Knowledge Graph](lightprof.md) - Lightweight KGQA framework that uses a small model with structure-aware prompt learning over KG triples. (neocortex: watch · LLM: retrieval)
* [RuleRAG: Rule-Guided Retrieval-Augmented Generation with Language Models for Question Answering](rulerag.md) - Adds logical rules, many derived from KGs, as in-context guidance for both the retriever and the generator. (neocortex: watch · LLM: both)
* [StructRAG: Boosting Knowledge Intensive Reasoning of LLMs via Inference-time Hybrid Information Structurization](structrag.md) - Chooses a structure type for the task, reconstructs documents into that structure at inference, and answers from it. (neocortex: watch · LLM: both)
* [StructuGraphRAG: Structured Document-Informed Knowledge Graphs for Retrieval-Augmented Generation](structugraphrag.md) - Builds structured-document-informed KGs for RAG (AAAI 2024 Spring Symposium). (neocortex: watch · LLM: both)
* [Call Me When Necessary: LLMs can Efficiently and Faithfully Reason over Structured Environments](readi.md) - Readi: the LLM proposes a reasoning path over a KG or table and edits it only when instantiation fails. (neocortex: skip · LLM: both)
* [CancerKG.ORG A Web-scale, Interactive, Verifiable Knowledge Graph-LLM Hybrid for Assisting with Optimal Cancer Treatment and Care](cancerkg.md) - A cancer KG-LLM hybrid populated from peer-reviewed literature, used as a RAG guardrail. (neocortex: skip · LLM: both)
* [Enhancing Textbook Question Answering with Knowledge Graph-Augmented Large Language Models](textbook-qa-kg.md) - KG-augmented LLMs for textbook question answering (ACML 2024). Title and authors confirmed; no abstract or full text was retrievable. (neocortex: skip · LLM: both)
* [Generative Subgraph Retrieval for Knowledge Graph-Grounded Dialog Generation](dialoggsr.md) - Generates relevant KG subgraphs token by token with a pretrained LM, instead of a dense triple retriever. (neocortex: skip · LLM: both)
* [Graph-constrained Reasoning: Faithful Reasoning on Knowledge Graphs with Large Language Models](gcr.md) - GCR constrains LLM decoding with a KG-Trie index of reasoning paths, so generated paths exist in the KG. (neocortex: skip · LLM: both)
* [How to Make LLMs Strong Node Classifiers?](lm-node-classifiers.md) - Makes an off-the-shelf LM (Flan-T5) match GNN node classifiers by enriching its input with retrieved neighbours and pruning classes with a small GNN. (neocortex: skip · LLM: both)
* [Inference Scaled GraphRAG: Improving Multi Hop Question Answering on Knowledge Graphs](inference-scaled-graphrag.md) - Adds inference-time compute to LLM graph traversal: sequential deep chain-of-thought traversal plus parallel majority voting over sampled trajectories. (neocortex: skip · LLM: both)
* [Intelligent question answering for water conservancy project inspection driven by knowledge graph and large language model collaboration](water-conservancy-kgqa.md) - Hydroengineering inspection QA where the LLM clarifies a query along a path guided by a knowledge graph (LHB 2024; abstract only). (neocortex: skip · LLM: both)
* [MEG: Medical Knowledge-Augmented Large Language Models for Question Answering](meg.md) - Maps KG embeddings into an LLM through a lightweight mapping network, parameter-efficiently. (neocortex: skip · LLM: both)
* [RD-P: A Trustworthy Retrieval-Augmented Prompter with Knowledge Graphs for LLMs](rd-p.md) - Trains one pre-trained-LM prompter whose retriever finds KG reasoning paths and whose discriminator scores their credibility, then builds a prompt for the LLM (CIKM 2024; abstract only). (neocortex: skip · LLM: both)
