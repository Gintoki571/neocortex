# Benchmarks

* [GraphRAG-Bench](graphrag-bench.md) - Benchmark of about 4,000 questions over two corpora (Gutenberg novels and medical guidelines) asking when graph structure helps RAG across four task levels. (neocortex: adopt · LLM: both)
* [MetaQA](metaqa.md) - Multi-hop KB question answering benchmark (1-, 2- and 3-hop) over the WikiMovies knowledge base, with Vanilla, NTM-paraphrased and audio variants; about 400K questions in total. (neocortex: adopt · LLM: none)
* [MetaQA-3 (3-hop split)](metaqa-3hop.md) - The 3-hop split of MetaQA: 142,744 template questions over the WikiMovies KB, requiring three-hop logical reasoning (AAAI 2018). (neocortex: adopt · LLM: none)
* [MultiHop-RAG](multihop-rag.md) - RAG benchmark of 2,556 multi-hop news queries with gold evidence sentences, ground-truth answers and null (unanswerable) queries. (neocortex: adopt · LLM: both)
* [ComplexWebQuestions (CWQ)](cwq.md) - 34,689 complex questions over Freebase, generated by composing SPARQL queries from WebQuestionsSP with conjunctions, superlatives, comparatives and compositions. (neocortex: adapt · LLM: none)
* [DIGIMON (graph RAG unified-framework benchmark)](digimon.md) - Study that places graph-based RAG methods in one framework and compares them on 11 QA datasets, from specific to abstract questions. (neocortex: adapt · LLM: both)
* [KQA Pro](kqa-pro.md) - 117,970 natural-language complex KBQA questions with KoPL programs and SPARQL queries, used to diagnose compositional reasoning skills (ACL 2022). (neocortex: adapt · LLM: none)
* [PolyBench (PolyG benchmark)](polyg.md) - GraphRAG benchmark of 1,200 questions over three knowledge graphs (DBLP physics academia, Goodreads literature, Amazon e-commerce) covering a four-class question taxonomy, introduced with the PolyG method. (neocortex: adapt · LLM: retrieval)
* [CRUD-RAG](crud-rag.md) - Chinese RAG benchmark with four application types (Create, Read, Update, Delete) that scores every RAG component, not only the LLM. (neocortex: watch · LLM: both)
* [FACTKG](factkg.md) - 108,674 fact-checking claims over DBpedia triples, labelled SUPPORTED or REFUTED, with five reasoning types and written and colloquial styles (ACL 2023). (neocortex: watch · LLM: none)
* [GrailQA](grailqa.md) - Large-scale Freebase KBQA dataset with 64,331 questions split to test three levels of generalization (i.i.d., compositional, zero-shot). (neocortex: watch · LLM: none)
* [LC-QuAD](lc-quad.md) - 5,000-question gold KBQA dataset (4,000 train, 1,000 test) pairing questions with SPARQL queries over DBpedia 2016-04 (ISWC 2017). (neocortex: watch · LLM: none)
* [LC-QuAD 2.0](lc-quad-2.md) - 30,000 complex natural-language questions with paraphrases and SPARQL queries over Wikidata and DBpedia 2018 (ISWC 2019). (neocortex: watch · LLM: none)
* [Mintaka](mintaka.md) - End-to-end multilingual complex QA dataset with 20,000 English question-answer pairs, annotated with Wikidata entities. (neocortex: watch · LLM: none)
* [TutorQA](tutorqa.md) - Expert-verified NLP education QA benchmark of 1,200 questions in six concept-graph tasks, introduced in the Graphusion paper (arXiv 2407.10794). (neocortex: watch · LLM: both)
* [UltraDomain](ultradomain.md) - Domain-specific long-context QA dataset, introduced with MemoRAG (the dataset link in the README points to the MemoRAG repo). (neocortex: watch · LLM: both)
* [SimpleQuestions](simplequestions.md) - Large single-relation question-answering dataset of 100k questions over a knowledge base, used with Memory Networks. (neocortex: skip · LLM: none)
* [WebQSP](webqsp.md) - WebQuestionsSP: SPARQL semantic-parse labels for 4,737 WebQuestions over Freebase, showing that parse labels beat answer-only training (ACL 2016). (neocortex: skip · LLM: none)
* [WebQuestions](webq.md) - 5,810 Google Suggest questions answered over Freebase, the original weakly supervised KBQA benchmark (EMNLP 2013). (neocortex: skip · LLM: none)
